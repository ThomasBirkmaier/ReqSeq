**Save the lists you need as perspectives, and let the command prompt guide every step.**

- **Perspectives.** Save a list with its filter, grouping and sort as a perspective, open it again with Ctrl/Cmd+K V as its own buffer, and see up to ten perspectives with their counts on the overview. The modeline says "modified" while a perspective has changes. Perspectives and saved filters can be renamed and deleted, in the app as in the CLI, API and MCP.
- **One way to filter.** "Filter list" replaces the five filter commands: it asks the kind first, then the value, and lets you pick several types in one step. "New perspective" and "New saved filter" ask every step before they save, and "Save list as saved filter" keeps the conditions and sort of the shown list.
- **A command prompt that guides.** The empty command prompt shows nine fitting commands and "All commands…". Fixed values, such as kinds, properties, saved filters or packages, are offered as hits to choose, conditions and sorts are built step by step, an explanation line describes the marked command and the asked argument, and Ctrl+H shows its full help. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Enter at the search words to search only in the fields you choose.
- **Swipe between buffers.** A buffer switch slides the old buffer out and the new one in, and a two-finger swipe on the trackpad follows your fingers to the previous or next buffer. Back and Forward that macOS sends as a swipe, for example from a mouse, switch the buffer too.
- **Hints, graphs and criteria.** The hint list is grouped by kind with counts and one line per hint, the element and metamodel graphs can be copied as Mermaid, and the element page shows a reference for each acceptance criterion, with "Copy criterion reference".
