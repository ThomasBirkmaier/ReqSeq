**Save the lists you need as perspectives, and let the command prompt guide every step.**

- **Perspectives.** Save a list with its filter, grouping and sort as a perspective, open it again with Ctrl/Cmd+K V as its own buffer, and see up to ten perspectives with their counts on the overview. The modeline says "modified" while a perspective has changes, and perspectives and saved filters can be renamed and deleted from the command prompt; the CLI, API and MCP save and list perspectives too.
- **One way to filter.** "Filter list" replaces the five filter commands: it asks the kind first, then the value, and lets you pick several types in one step. "New perspective" and "New saved filter" ask every step before they save, and "Save list as saved filter" keeps the conditions and sort of the shown list.
- **A command prompt that guides.** The empty command prompt shows nine fitting commands and "All commands…". Fixed values, such as kinds, properties, saved filters or packages, are offered as hits to choose, conditions and sorts are built step by step, an explanation line describes the marked command and the asked argument, and Ctrl+H shows its full help.
- **Search in chosen fields.** Press Ctrl/Cmd+Enter at the search words to search only in the fields you choose, such as the title or a single property; the list of search hits names them under its heading.
- **Switching buffers that follows your fingers.** A buffer switch slides the old buffer out and the new one in, and a two-finger swipe on the trackpad, or the Back and Forward buttons of the mouse, switches to the previous or next buffer. The hint list is grouped by kind with counts, and the element and metamodel graphs can be copied as Mermaid.
