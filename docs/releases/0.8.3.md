**A real editor for the text of an element.**

- **Write comfortably.** Format a selection as bold, italic, code or link, tick tasks with a click, indent and drag list items, and move through and change tables with the keyboard. Find and replace works inside the text.
- **See what you write.** Images from your repository appear right in the text, pasted or dropped content arrives as Markdown, and sections fold under their headings. The text stands in a calm, easy-to-read column.
- **Graph.** Each node shows its verification status and progress, and hovering a link names its kind.
- **Command line.** Search hits appear while you type, and paths complete as you type them, also in Open workspace.
- **Switch pages faster.** On macOS, Cmd+[ and Cmd+] go back and forward between the pages you opened.
