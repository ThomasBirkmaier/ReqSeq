**Smoother navigation and safer files.**

- **Command line.** It now rises as a card over a dimmed background, and Go to puts the element whose title matches exactly first.
- **Search.** Each search hit shows where it was found, with a snippet of the text around the match.
- **Move with keys and mouse.** Ctrl+N and Ctrl+P move from field to field in an element, and the back and forward buttons of your mouse switch between the pages you opened.
- **Nothing lost after a crash.** If the window crashes, ReqSeq reloads it and brings back what you had not saved yet.
- **Safer files.** ReqSeq refuses links and image paths that would lead outside your repositories or to a network drive.
