**The first release you can download.**

- **Ready to unpack.** ReqSeq comes as an app for macOS, Windows and Linux, together with the `reqseq` command line. It needs no installer and no internet connection.
- **Messages that help.** Every problem and error has a code, its cause and what to do about it.
- **Command reference.** A complete list of commands, made from ReqSeq itself, so it always matches your version.
- **Export.** Export the data of a workspace with one element per line, easy for other programs to read.
