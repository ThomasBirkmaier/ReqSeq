**Exchange requirements with other tools through ReqIF.**

- **Import.** Bring requirements from other requirements tools into a workspace through ReqIF, their common exchange format. You decide once how their fields map to yours, and ReqSeq keeps that mapping for the next delivery.
- **Updated deliveries.** When a new version of the same document arrives, ReqSeq matches it to what you imported before and updates it instead of adding copies. Formatted text and images come along.
- **Answer back.** Export your statements on the supplier's requirements as ReqIF and send them back.
- **Two elements side by side.** Split the window, and the other side previews what you mark. Alt+P and Alt+N walk through the pages you opened.
