# Anemometer

The **anemometer** measures [gusts](https://example.com/gusts "Gust page") with `pulse counting`, as [Show a dial](reqseq:17400000-0000-4000-8000-0000000000a2?version=n1:0000000000000000) shows.

- It reports every second.
- It warns of a storm above 20 m/s.
