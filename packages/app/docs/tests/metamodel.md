Part of [Tests](../tests.md). The behaviour is in [Metamodel (SR-130)](../metamodel.md).

- `test/metamodel.spec.ts` runs the metamodel on `fixtures/SR-130/metamodel` and compares it with `reqseq metamodel get`, mapped by the test itself (`[SR-130/AK-dp8b]`: "Show metamodel" by `sm` and Enter, the modeline "Metamodel" and "5 types", no result buffer, not offered on the metamodel; `[SR-130/AK-s795]`: every box's name, "abstract", prefix and properties with the inherited ones marked, every label line, every parent above its subtypes, an arrowhead on every curve, nothing cut; `[SR-130/AK-j585]`: Based on, Traces to, Refines and Concerns at "Any type" with the open side, Blocks (an empty list) not drawn; `[SR-130/AK-g9gk]`: Down, Right, Page Up, Down, Home, and Ctrl/Cmd+T between graph and text with the selected type kept; `[SR-130/AK-y6rs]`: every row of the text as the CLI's output says, mapped by the test; `[SR-130/AK-cmt2]`: the MCP server's `metamodel_get` equals the CLI's output, and the text shows it). - `test/metamodel-limits.spec.ts` runs the metamodel as text on `fixtures/SR-152/limits` (the two limits of Derives from, none at Based on) and `fixtures/SR-171/max-items` ("list of text · at most 3") (#847).
