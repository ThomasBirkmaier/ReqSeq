# Displays block (`src/displays/`)

The display models the app and the HTML export share (ADR-0019): one pure builder per display, from data in the shape of command outputs, never from the store. The renderers only lay a model out. Exported as `@reqseq/core/displays` (`index.ts`). A module here imports only types (such as the output types of `catalog.ts`), other display models and the import-free modules `graph/words.ts` and `problem-titles.ts`, so the app's sandboxed renderer bundle never pulls in `node:*`; the `no-restricted-imports` block for `packages/core/src/displays/**` in `eslint.config.js` enforces it.

- Hint list (`hint-list.ts`, SR-026 AK4): `hintListDisplay(output, views)` takes the output of `hint.list` and the names of the variants by UUID, and gives the total, the number of hints on the page, `incomplete`, and a group per entry of `kinds` in its order with its count and the title of its kind (`problemTitle`, SR-224 AK6; its code for a kind without one). Each group holds the hints of the page of its kind in the order of the output, each with its element (`shownId`, else its identifier, UUID or file), the element's title and name (`shownIdTitle`), the reason (`hintReason`: of a count its relation kind or property, target type, count and limit; of outdated evidence the view; of a relation to a resolved target its kind and that target; else the start of the message, `messageStart`, after the relation kind and the target where there are any), the message, and its target, the element by UUID and repository (its own repository, not that of an evidence record). The app's `hint-list.ts` adds the buffer a row opens, the technical values of "Show details" and the folding.

Tests: `src/displays/hint-list.test.ts` (100 % per file, ADR-0016); the AKs are tested where they are observed, in `packages/app/src/renderer/hint-list.test.ts` and `packages/app/test/hint-list.spec.ts`.
